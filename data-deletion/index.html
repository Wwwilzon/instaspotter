Data Deletion Instructions

Spotter (Instagram: @spotter.community) · Effective 4 November 2026

If you commented a keyword on one of our Instagram posts or messaged @spotter.community, our app may hold a small amount of data about you. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what it is. Here is how to have it deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Instagram and send a direct message to @spotter.community from the account you used.
  2. Write DELETE MY DATA.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm when it's done.

You don't need to give any other details. We use your Instagram account to find your records.

What we delete

  • Every reply record linked to your Instagram-scoped user ID (keyword, post ID, comment ID, time).
  • Any pending offer waiting for your YES.
  • If you ask, our side of the conversation with you in the @spotter.community inbox.

Technical logs are deleted automatically by our hosting provider after a few days (3 days on our current plan), so they will be gone by the time we confirm.

Good to know

  • Your comments stay on Instagram until you delete them yourself. You can delete a comment, or your side of a conversation, in the Instagram app at any time.
  • After deletion we no longer know that we already sent you a resource. If you comment the keyword again later, you may get the private reply again.
  • Data Instagram holds about you is covered by Instagram's Privacy Policy and can be managed in your Instagram settings.
