Privacy Policy

Spotter (Instagram: @spotter.community) · Effective 4 November 2026

This policy covers the Spotter Meta app, which we use to run our own Instagram account, @spotter.community. It explains what happens to your data when you interact with that account, mainly when you comment a keyword (for example FILE) on one of our posts to get a free firearm-safety education resource by direct message.

Who we are

Spotter runs the Instagram account @spotter.community, which shares free firearm-safety education. We are responsible for the data described here. To contact us about privacy, send a direct message to @spotter.community on Instagram.

What happens when you comment a keyword

  1. When someone comments on one of our posts, Instagram sends our app a notification. It contains the comment text, the comment ID, the post ID, and the commenter's Instagram-scoped user ID and username.
  2. Our app checks the comment text for the keyword. If the keyword is there, we send you one private reply on Instagram asking whether you want the resource.
  3. If you answer YES, we send you a link to the resource (a PDF).
  4. Some resources may be offered only to followers. For those, after you answer YES, our app asks Instagram once whether you follow @spotter.community. If you don't, we send you one message asking you to follow first.

We do not store the text of your comment, the text of your messages, or your username. Our app reads them only to decide whether to reply, and then discards them.

The result of the follow check (whether you follow us) is used once and not stored.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone for their own purposes.
  • We don't use it for advertising or profiling, and we don't add you to any mailing list.
  • We don't message you beyond the replies described above: one private reply, at most one "follow first" message, and the link.
  • We don't sell firearms, ammunition or accessories, and nothing in these messages is an offer to buy or sell anything. The resources are free.

Services we rely on

  • Meta / Instagram, which delivers comments and messages (Instagram Privacy Policy).
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our app and stores the records above (Cloudflare Privacy Policy). Cloudflare may process data in the United States and other countries.
  • Google, which hosts the PDF resources. If you open the link, Google handles that request (Google Privacy Policy).
  • GitHub, which hosts this page (GitHub Privacy Statement).

Our own account data

The same app also publishes posts on @spotter.community and reads that account's statistics (follower count and per-post likes, comments, saves, shares, reach and views). These are totals; they contain no information about individual people.

Legal basis (for people in the EU/EEA and UK)

We send the resource because you asked for it by commenting the keyword and answering YES. We keep the reply record because of our legitimate interest in not messaging you more than once for the same post.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to its use. Send a direct message to @spotter.community. See Data deletion instructions for the steps. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how long we keep it, we will update this page and its effective date.
